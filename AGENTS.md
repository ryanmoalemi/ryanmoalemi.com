# Agent notes

## Page section order

On every page, put the most engaging sections immediately under the intro or hero: photos, projects, galleries, tools, and the main story.

Order the remaining sections from most interesting to least interesting. End with notes, disclosures, and fine print.

Keep each photo credit with its photo. Move existing sections only. Do not rewrite or remove copy to change the order. The full hero title must stay above the fold on desktop and mobile.
