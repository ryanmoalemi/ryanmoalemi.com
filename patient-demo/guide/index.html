What’s due

Password for every account, if you type it: harbor-demo. The header can switch roles without signing out. This browser keeps its own copy of the sandbox. Reset demo data in the banner restores the seed and the mission guide. A Guide button in the corner walks the same story.

  1. From the login screen, continue as Coordinator. The week list already has Subject 002 ready for infusion, Subject 001's collection today, Subject 003's day-14 visit, an open serious-event clock, and an IRB date inside 30 days.
  2. Optional full setup: New study from a template. Choose autologous CAR-T. The page states the 15-year follow-up before you save. Enroll a fictional subject and open the label.
  3. Open Subject 002 from the dashboard. On infusion, choose Demo: scan a different bag. The step blocks and names both IDs.
  4. Choose Use this subject's ID, then Record scan. Switch to Cell Lab / Pharmacist and sign product verification. Switch to Principal Investigator and sign clinical verification. Infusion completes and the 15-year calendar appears.
  5. Switch back to Coordinator. Log a grade 3 cytokine release syndrome event and watch the sponsor-notification clock. Sign Confirm sponsor notified.
  6. Open Inspection export and use Print / save PDF. Subject 003 is the long timeline if you want follow-up and a past CRS already on the record.

Monitor (read-only) can open every record and the export, and cannot change them. Regulatory can update IRB and IBC dates. The allogeneic template cannot be signed. Subject 004 is on AUTO-MSC-004 so you can compare a 2-year stem-cell calendar with the 15-year CAR-T calendar.

Terms

Autologous
The cells come from the same person who will receive them.
Chain of identity
The 15-character ID that ties this person to this bag through every handoff.
Chain of custody
The record of who held the product at each step, from collection to infusion.
Apheresis
The collection visit. Blood is drawn, the cells are kept, and the rest is returned.
COA
Certificate of analysis. The manufacturer’s release results for this bag.
IBC
Institutional biosafety committee. It reviews gene-modified products before the site uses them.
IRB
Institutional review board. It approves the protocol and the consent form.
CRS
Cytokine release syndrome. A fever-and-blood-pressure reaction that can follow CAR-T.
ICANS
The neurologic syndrome that can follow CAR-T, graded separately from CRS.
21 CFR 1271.90
The rule that exempts autologous cells from donor screening and testing when they are labeled for autologous use only.
21 CFR 312.64(b)
The investigator tells the sponsor about a serious adverse event immediately.
