A site-side tracker for autologous cell therapy and stem cell trials

This demo shows how a clinical site can follow a fictional autologous cell-therapy patient from enrollment through chain of identity, custody handoffs, a two-person infusion check, safety events, and long-term follow-up. One of the seeded studies is a stem cell (MSC) protocol. The people and results are made up.
